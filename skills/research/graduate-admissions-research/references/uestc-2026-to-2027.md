# UESTC 2026→2027 admissions case study

This reference records durable mechanics from a September 2026 comparison of electronic-information programmes at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC). It is an example, not a permanent prediction for later cohorts.

## Cohort normalization

The user was preparing to register in autumn 2026, sit the written exam at the end of 2026, and enter in 2027. “Last year” therefore meant the completed 2026-entry cycle: registration/written exam in 2025 and re-exam/admission in spring 2026.

The live 2027 catalogue route existed but rendered `2027年招生目录尚未开放！`. This was stronger evidence than assuming publication from the calendar or copying the 2026 catalogue.

## Dynamic catalogue endpoints

The catalogue index used a tree plus iframes:

```text
https://yzbm.uestc.edu.cn/zsml/sszsml/index/2026
POST /zsml/getTreeData   nd=2026&zslb=1
/zsml/sszsml/zsmlZy/2026/<college-code>
/zsml/sszsml/zsmlYjfx/2026/<college-code>/<programme-code>?ksfs=21
```

Rendered programme pages exposed:

- programme and direction;
- planned intake including recommended admissions and special plans;
- initial-exam subjects;
- re-exam subjects;
- notes about special plans.

Do not search only by programme name. For example, `085400 电子信息` used different fourth-subject papers in different colleges/directions.

## Quota normalization

Two numbers that looked similar were not interchangeable:

- catalogue `拟招生人数`: based on the previous cycle and commonly includes recommended admission and special plans;
- re-exam notice `拟公开招考人数`: the more relevant ordinary public-exam quota after recommended admissions.

The comparison table used the latter and labelled it explicitly.

## Official score-line source

The school published a single historical college-line page linking full tables for multiple years. The rendered rows were easier and safer to normalize than collecting screenshots from many third-party posts.

Three-year lines were used to show volatility, not to draw a linear forecast. Large jumps in formerly low-cutoff directions were treated as applicant migration/competition risk.

## Attachment recovery patterns

### Embedded viewer

One college's announcement had no visible attachment anchor, but DOM inspection found:

```html
<iframe src="/system/resource/pdfjs/viewer.html?file=%2F__local%2F...pdf">
```

Fetching the decoded `/__local/...pdf` path returned the real PDF directly.

### CAPTCHA download handler

Other announcements linked through:

```text
/system/_content/download.jsp?urltype=news.DownloadAttachUrl&owner=...&wbfileid=...
```

Raw `curl` saved a short HTML JavaScript/browser-check page under a `.pdf` filename. The working path was:

1. open the attachment handler in a real browser;
2. let the site browser check complete;
3. complete the ordinary four-digit attachment CAPTCHA;
4. enable browser download behavior and submit;
5. wait until no `.crdownload` remains;
6. copy the resulting PDF to stable storage;
7. verify with `file`/`pdfinfo` before parsing.

The durable lesson is not that `curl` is broken; it is that downloaded content must be type-checked and browser-mediated attachment flows may be required.

## Mechanical statistics

The PDFs were converted with:

```bash
pdftotext -layout admitted-list.pdf admitted-list.txt
```

A script then filtered exact programme name, direction code, and study mode before computing count, minimum, median, mean, and maximum.

Manual inspection was still required for:

- special-plan notes;
- candidates below the ordinary score line;
- full-time vs non-full-time;
- direction codes appearing in separate columns;
- adjusted final quotas.

## Interpretation lessons

- Re-exam line is a threshold, not a safe score.
- A near-line admit can be explained by an exceptional re-exam when initial and re-exam are weighted 50/50.
- Median admitted initial score is usually a better planning anchor than the minimum.
- An eight-person direction may be riskier than a fifty-person direction despite a lower prior cutoff.
- A lower-cutoff college is not automatically preferable if switching examination subjects late destroys months of preparation.
- Joint-training language can materially affect location and supervision and belongs in the comparison table.

## Recommendation structure that worked

The final output narrowed the field instead of dumping every possible college:

1. balanced/default option;
2. strongest project-fit but small-cohort option;
3. location-dependent alternative;
4. high-score reach option;
5. conditional option requiring an examination-subject switch;
6. explicit non-priority options with reasons.

This made the research actionable while preserving uncertainty until the new catalogue opened.
